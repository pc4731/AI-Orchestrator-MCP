When designing or building UI, meet WCAG 2.1 AA:
- Sufficient color contrast (>= 4.5:1 for body text); never convey meaning by color alone.
- Full keyboard operability with visible focus states; logical tab order.
- Semantic HTML and ARIA roles/labels; alt text for meaningful images.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme.
- Accessible forms: labels tied to inputs, clear error messages, adequate hit targets.
Call out any accessibility trade-offs explicitly in your output.
